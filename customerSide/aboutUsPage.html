KumaMori

A Charm of Comfort and Protection

KumaMori is more than a brand. It is a community that celebrates kindness, creativity, and self-protection. Carry your charm proudly. Carry KumaMori with you.

Inspired by "Kuma" (bear) and "Omamori" (Japanese charm), we blend strength, warmth, and good energy into every product. Our pieces combine style, meaning, and everyday function to help you feel confident and cared for wherever you go.

Vision

KumaMori envisions building a trusted presence within campus communities and nearby areas in Metro Manila by offering stylish and functional self-defense keychains that promote personal safety and self-expression among female Gen Zs and LGBT commuters.

Mission

KumaMori aims to empower commuters, especially Gen Z female and LGBT individuals, by promoting personal safety and self-expression through discreet self-defense tools. Over the course of its operations, the company seeks to build a trustworthy presence within its community, create meaningful connections, foster loyalty, and strengthen sustainable sales growth while making a positive impact on everyday lives.
